Project Features & Capabilities

Key components built into this application
  • Gamified Habit Tracking & Daily Quests

    Organize habits across Coding, Fitness, Study, Mindset & Health with quest difficulty tiers (E-Rank to S-Rank) and instant XP rewards.

  • Uchiha Clan Rank Progression

    Level up from 1-Tomoe Genin and 2-Tomoe Chūnin up to Mangekyō Sharingan and S-Rank Uchiha Sovereign as you conquer daily targets.

  • Shinobi Spirit Familiars

    Unlock companions like Kage Wolf, Homura Fire Fox, Karasu Crow, Ryuujin Dragon, and Susanoo providing passive XP multipliers.

  • Atomic Streak Flame & Calendar Heatmaps

    Mathematically accurate streak calculations, dynamic month traversal, daily reflection logs, and habit consistency analytics.

  • Analytics Dashboard & RPG Attribute Radar

    7-day completion rates, 30-day consistency metrics, category breakdowns, and dynamic RPG character stat radars.

  • Isolated Multi-Tenant Security & Payments

    Encrypted authentication with JWT/bcrypt, isolated user vaults, and Razorpay payment gateway integration.

How It Was Built (AI-Assisted Engineering & Vision)

System architecture, prompt orchestration & creative direction
AI-Assisted Development with 100% Custom Direction & Architecture

This project was engineered through an AI-first development workflow. While the entire codebase was generated and iteratively implemented using advanced AI tools, every single creative, architectural, and functional decision was directed by me — from the anime/Uchiha dark crimson aesthetic, UI/UX structure and component layout (what element goes where), to feature workflows (how habits, streaks, and ranks operate) and the underlying gamification mechanics.

01
Theme & Creative Direction

Conceptualized the dark crimson Uchiha aesthetic, Sharingan eye rank progression tiers, and anime psychological gamification model.

02
UI/UX & Layout Architecture

Designed the complete interface structure, defining where every card, heatmap, quest list, and familiar card is positioned for intuitive flow.

03
Feature Logic & Workflows

Formulated the mathematical streak algorithms, XP scaling formula, quest difficulty tiers (E to S-Rank), and companion multipliers.

04
AI Orchestration & Deployment

Guided AI with modular prompt engineering to generate clean Next.js/React code, followed by rigorous debugging and live Vercel deployment.
