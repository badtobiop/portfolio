Project Features & Capabilities

Key components built into this application
  • Gamified Habit Tracking & Daily Quests

    Organize habits across Coding, Fitness, Study, Mindset & Health with quest difficulty tiers (E-Rank to S-Rank) and instant XP rewards.

  • Uchiha Clan Rank Progression

    Level up from 1-Tomoe Genin and 2-Tomoe Chūnin up to Mangekyō Sharingan and S-Rank Uchiha Sovereign as you conquer daily targets.

  • Shinobi Spirit Familiars

    Unlock companions like Kage Wolf, Homura Fire Fox, Karasu Crow, Ryuujin Dragon, and Susanoo providing passive XP multipliers.

  • Atomic Streak Flame & Calendar Heatmaps

    Mathematically accurate streak calculations, dynamic month traversal, daily reflection logs, and habit consistency analytics.

  • Analytics Dashboard & RPG Attribute Radar

    7-day completion rates, 30-day consistency metrics, category breakdowns, and dynamic RPG character stat radars.

  • Isolated Multi-Tenant Security & Payments

    Encrypted authentication with JWT/bcrypt, isolated user vaults, and Razorpay payment gateway integration.
